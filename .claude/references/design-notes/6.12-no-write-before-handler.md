# §6.12 — design notes (no write before handler)

> Full rationale, history, measurements and rot guards behind the condensed rule §6.12 in `.claude/rules/`. Read on demand when changing this area. Verbatim from the pre-split `performance.md` (2026-11-25).

**§6.12** **Nothing is written for a job between its claim and its handler.** Both worker modes used to commit before the handler ran: single-worker mode saved the "Processing" `JobLog` on its own (one statement, one commit), and dispatcher mode issued a guarded ownership `UPDATE` and then that same insert (two statements, two commits), for every job. Neither is needed up front. **The "Processing" row is deferred** (`DeferredJobLog`): the job monitor's first flush writes it for a job that runs that long, otherwise it rides the finalizing write, and whichever asks first takes it, so it is written once. It keeps the receiving worker's id and the instant the worker took the job. **The dispatcher's ownership check is skipped for a fresh claim** (`WarpDispatcherWorker.ConfirmOwnership`): `StaleJobRecovery` reclaims a job only when its `LastKeepAlive` is a whole `InvisibilityTimeout` old, so a job received within `InvisibilityTimeout / 2 - CancellationCheckInterval` of its claim cannot have been reclaimed; the other half of the timeout is margin for two servers' clocks. A job that waited in the channel longer than that still gets the guarded renewal (`RenewClaim`), which is what keeps a reclaimed prefetched job from running twice (`DispatcherPrefetchTests`). Consequences, all accepted: a short job's "Processing" row appears when it completes; a job that dies with its process before its first flush has none; and in dispatcher mode the row carries the worker GROUP's id from the claim until the first keep-alive stamps the worker's, so several running jobs can share one `CurrentWorkerId` and readers must not key a dictionary on it (`DashboardStatsService` uses `DistinctBy`). The queue-wait increments go to the counter buffer at receipt, since the claim that made the job ours is already committed. Do not move either write back in front of the handler. Rot guards: `WorkerExecutionIntegrationTests.GivenLongRunningHandler_WhileItRuns_ThenItsProcessingLogAndItsWorkerAppear` and `GivenShortHandler_WhenItCompletes_ThenItsProcessingLogIsWrittenWithTheCompletion` (both modes), `ServerQueryTests.GetServers_SeveralRunningJobsUnderOneId_StillLoads`, and `WorkerPathLockstepTests` for the two modes agreeing on the log rows.
