**§6.24** **Perf-CI gates that are report-only until calibrated (TODO).** The performance workflow's comparer (`PerfCompare.RenderScenario`) shows these and fails nothing, because their measurement is new or was reworked in measurement version 2 and has not been calibrated by comparing a commit with itself on CI (three no-op runs, the way the statement and WAL lines were): **(1)** every SQL Server per-job row (statements and buffers now come from Query Store; `reportOnly` for `IsSqlServer && !perSecond`), **(2)** the `TwoServersUnderLoad`, `RecurringJobs` and `BackgroundLoad` scenarios (`[CiScenario(Calibrated = false)]`), **(3)** DB ms/job on both providers (the provisional +25% PostgreSQL line was removed: message routing swung ~60% between identical builds).
