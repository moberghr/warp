# §8.32 — design notes (dropped records)

> Full rationale, history, measurements and rot guards behind the condensed rule §8.32 in `.claude/rules/`. Read on demand when changing this area. Verbatim from the pre-split `project-specific.md` (2026-11-24).

**§8.32** **Dropped-record visibility.** The lossy recording pipelines (adapters §8.19, endpoints §8.21, client §8.27) drop records when their bounded channel is full and increment an always-on OTel meter (`warp.{adapter|endpoint|client}.records_dropped`) — reliable because the drop signal never rides the lossy channel it reports on, but only observable through an OTel backend. To make a saturated pipeline visible **in-box** (the whole point of the dashboard): each drop site also calls `DroppedRecordCounters.Track(DropPipeline, n)` (a single interlocked add — safe on the recording-failure path) into a process-global counter; a per-process `DroppedRecordReporter<TContext>` (`BackgroundService`, **not** a locked `IServerTask` — the counters are per-process, each process reports its own; 30s cadence, **no DB work when nothing dropped** so it's a good neighbour) drains them and folds each delta to the durable tiered stat `warpsys:records-dropped:{pipeline}` (`DroppedRecordKeys`, disjoint `warpsys` first-segment namespace §8.6/§8.19; classified/rolled by the generic `MetricTiers` reader §8.30). `DashboardStatsService` sums the last-24h buckets (windowed, so the tile self-clears) into `DashboardStatistics.{Adapter|Endpoint|Client}RecordsDropped`, surfaced as a red-when-nonzero **Dropped (24h)** tile on the dashboard. The reporter also raises a **throttled** (per-pipeline 5-min cooldown) `WarpEventType.RecordsDropped`=8 (`RecordsDroppedEvent`, Warning) post-commit through the notifier seam (§8.25), so a saturated pipeline is alertable in Slack/email **without OTel**.
