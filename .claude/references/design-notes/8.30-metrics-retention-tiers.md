# §8.30 — design notes (metrics retention tiers)

> Full rationale, history, measurements and rot guards behind the condensed rule §8.30 in `.claude/rules/`. Read on demand when changing this area. Verbatim from the pre-split `project-specific.md` (2026-11-24).

**§8.30** **Metrics retention tiers** make every time-bucketed `Statistic` series multi-resolution: **fine (5-min) → hourly → daily**, downsampled by rollup instead of the old delete-only prune. **Explicit tier marker** `MetricTiers` (`Warp.Core.Services`, `internal`): trailing suffix `:{m5|h1|d1}:{stamp}` (`yyyy-MM-dd-HH-mm` / `-HH` / day) — chosen over inferring resolution from stamp shape so per-family parsers stay fixed-length + disjoint (§8.6/§8.19). Applies to the hist/pcth families with a windowed consumer or latency histogram — **`jobstat` + `qwait` emit the fine tier** (fine `hist` + a new tiered **`pcth`** latency histogram alongside the untouched lifetime `pct`, for windowed percentiles); **`stats:`/adapter/endpoint/clientevent/errorgroup keep emitting unmarked hourly** and are rolled to daily by the generic pass (no emit change, strictly more history retained than the old delete). **Lifetime totals (`…:{token}`, no date) and lifetime `pct` are untouched.** **Emit change is inside each `*Keys.Build()`** (worker call sites just pass `MetricTiers.Suffix(Fine, now, FineResolutionMinutes)` instead of an hour string) — **zero `CounterAggregator` change** (it folds any key). **`StatisticRollup<TContext>`** is an `IServerTask` (§2.3, registered in `AddServerHostCore` beside `ExpirationCleanup`, `LockKey="warp:stat-rollup"`, cadence `WarpServerConfiguration.StatisticRollupInterval` default **10m**) — **family-agnostic**: `MetricTiers.TryClassifyKey` splits any key into (base, tier, bucket) from the trailing suffix (marked **or** legacy unmarked `:yyyy-MM-dd-HH`, so **pre-3.10 rows migrate to daily automatically**), then per pass it **sums-then-deletes** fine→hourly (older than `FineResolutionRetention` 6h) and hourly→daily (older than `HourlyStatisticsRetention` 7d — repurposed from a delete age), and prunes daily (older than `DailyStatisticsRetention` 90d, `null`=keep forever). **Crash-idempotent** inside the task lock transaction (source gone once its value lands in the parent); retention ordering (fine≪hourly≪daily) keeps targets/sources disjoint, with a guard that drops a colliding target so a pass can never update-then-delete the same row (only reachable if rollup was disabled past the hourly window). The **old delete-only hourly prune** in `ExpirationCleanup` (+ its `TryParseHourlySuffix`) is **removed**. **Readers (all history readers are tier-aware — daily is retained *and* read, not just stored):** `MetricTiers.TryClassifyKey` backs `DashboardStatsService.GetStatsHistory`/`GetCountersHistory`/`GetCounters` (tiered keys stay out of the counters *table*; fine buckets **down-bin to their hour**, grouped by base-key + hour), and each per-family chart parser — `AdapterCounterKeys`/`EndpointCounterKeys`/`ClientEventKeys.TryParseHistory` — accepts the marked tiers **and** legacy unmarked hourly and down-bins to the hour, so a rolled-to-daily window past the hourly retention still charts. Lifetime/`pct` reads untouched. **Config** on `WarpConfiguration`: `FineResolutionMinutes` (5; **must be >= 1**, validated fail-fast in `AddWarpServer` along with `fine < hourly < daily` ordering), `FineResolutionRetention`, `HourlyStatisticsRetention`, `DailyStatisticsRetention`; `StatisticRollupInterval` on `WarpServerConfiguration` (the rollup is the **only** pruner of time-bucketed rows now — `null` lets them accumulate). **No schema migration** — all tiers are new rows in the existing `Statistic`/`Counter` tables. This is the storage foundation SLO fast-burn reads its short window from. See `website/docs/features/metrics-retention.md`.
